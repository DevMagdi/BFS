## 2. Decisions required before product implementation

The owner-directed platform strategy is not yet customer-validated. The decisions below gate new domain analysis, production/customer-data use, and business interpretation; they do not block this bounded M4/M12 evidence workflow. M13 remains descriptive; M14 remains manual; M15/M16 and Core Financials remain experiments. Before domain-specific analytics or finance/customer-data use, accountable owners must decide:

1. **Product question and user:** Which repeated company-level decision or analytic question should be solved first, for whom, and what makes the current process inadequate?
2. **Domain and source owner:** Which single optional business domain is first? Who owns its facts, meanings, source authority, corrections, freshness and access?
3. **Organization scope:** Does the use case fit the existing single-company-per-tenant model? Do not infer multi-company consolidation or a fixed branch/location/department hierarchy.
4. **Finance policy:** For financial values or derived measures, an accountable finance/accounting owner must approve the data contract and each required interpretation/calculation rule before implementation or presentation as truth. See [`../architecture/FINANCIAL_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md`](../architecture/FINANCIAL_INTELLIGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md).
5. **Security and operations:** Confirm sensitive-data permissions, retention, residency, backup/restore, deployment/operator responsibilities and tests for the selected domain.
6. **Product validation:** Decide whether and how to validate the broader company-wide hypothesis. The existing founder discovery authorization in [`../FOUNDER_DECISION_MEMO.md`](../FOUNDER_DECISION_MEMO.md) is limited to the earlier project-acceptance hypothesis; this roadmap does not extend approval for outreach, customer-system access, recording or spending.

If these decisions are not made, the appropriate next step is owner-led domain/product discovery—not schema, API, formula, UI or infrastructure work.

## 3. Historical M9 candidate (context only)

This old M9 proposal is retained as context; it is not the current next step. The selected unnumbered Company Data Foundation increment is listed in the current implementation table above. M12 later implemented a narrower evidence registry for existing M4 workforce/project imports; M14 later implemented a separate, bounded finance-source intake. Neither is a general company-data platform, finance-policy approval, production/customer-data authorization, or approval for M15+ work.

**Bounded outcome proposed at M9:** Record one owner-approved domain data contract for one Company; preserve source identity, effective/as-of context, status, quality, lineage and authorized access; present one read-only observation or answer for one defined question. Use the existing PostgreSQL modular monolith, M4 staging/validation, M5 snapshot/hash/replay where applicable, and tenant/company isolation. Do not build a universal registry or schema before evidence demonstrates the need.

If the selected domain is finance, an initial slice may preserve and inspect exact, sourced observations only. No financial calculation, cross-currency arithmetic, verdict, forecast or ranking is permitted without owner-approved definitions, policy and golden examples. Preserve M8's intentionally incomplete behavior until that gate passes.

The first slice does not require AI. If AI is separately approved later, every response must follow **Evidence Before Answers — No evidence, no confident answer**. Any such capability must support local/on-prem inference; M9 selects no LLM, provider, runtime or vector store.

**Acceptance evidence to agree before implementation:**

- One named user question, owner, domain, Company scope and authoritative source/data contract.
- **Evidence Before Answers — No evidence, no confident answer:** any future AI answer must be:
  - **Evidence-grounded:** link material claims to authorized company data and source records.
  - **Traceable and verifiable:** expose supporting evidence and calculation inputs, versions and results.
  - **Transparent:** state assumptions, limitations, uncertainty and missing information.
  - **Permission-aware:** check authorization before retrieval and before returning an answer.
  - **Honest about insufficient data:** abstain from confident conclusions when evidence is incomplete or unreliable.
  - **Reproducible:** preserve relevant input, calculation, policy and evidence versions for important assessments/decisions.
- Explicit mapping and validation with visible missing/conflicting/estimated states; no silent promotion or zero-filling.
- Backend membership/permission checks, forced RLS, same-company foreign keys, least-privilege grants and PostgreSQL isolation tests.
- Reproducible read-only output with source lineage, scope, period, definition/code version, quality state and limitations.
- No unapproved formula or business verdict; decisions remain with authorized people.
- Clean migration/drift, regression/security tests, documented retention/runbook impacts and reviewed product acceptance.

**Outside this candidate:** all-company domain coverage, a general-purpose BI workbench, multi-company consolidation, a universal KPI/subject registry, a new datastore/microservice, broad live connectors, external actions, autonomous AI and a full finance/accounting engine. A minimal user experience needs its own product/scope decision.

## 6. MVP exclusion boundary and change control

The first MVP should demonstrate one valuable, governed company-level slice—not the entire company-wide vision. Unless an accountable owner separately approves otherwise, exclude:

- all-domain coverage, universal org structures, industry packs and configurations for fields a company does not use;
- multiple companies per tenant, group consolidation, intercompany accounting and cross-company analytics;
- full accounting/ERP/CRM/HR/payroll replacement, a general ledger, transaction posting, billing or payment processing;
- a universal KPI catalog, arbitrary formulas/SQL, a general semantic layer or all-purpose BI workspace;
- a live connector marketplace, real-time/automatic sync, arbitrary ERP mirroring, broad warehouse/lakehouse adoption or external writeback;
- any unapproved formula, accounting policy, threshold, currency conversion, rounding, cash forecast, profitability verdict or collection probability;
- AI-generated financial values, optimizer-generated policy, autonomous recommendation/approval/execution, high-impact actions, causal ROI claims or ungoverned outcome scoring;
- microservices, extra databases, workflow engines, brokers or a new analytics engine without demonstrated need;
- OR-Tools installation justified only by hypothetical future use cases.

These are first-slice exclusions, not a permanent statement that the product could never support such capabilities. Any addition requires explicit value, governance, architecture, security and owner approval. M10 remains synthetic-only and not production-ready; M12 adds no live connector or scheduler. Neither is a production-readiness claim.
