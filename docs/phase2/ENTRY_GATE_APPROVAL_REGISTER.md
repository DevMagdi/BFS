## How to update this register

Approval is recorded only when the authorized human role makes an explicit decision and a verifiable reference is supplied (for example, an owner-signed decision record committed to the repository). A technical project manager, developer, repository maintainer, app `owner`/`admin`, passing CI run, source profile, or feature flag is not automatically the Business Owner or an approval. Do not fill a person’s name, email, approval date, or decision from inference.

Use only the approval categories **APPROVED**, **RECOMMENDED**, **PENDING**, **BLOCKED**, or **NOT_APPLICABLE**. The `PENDING_*` strings below are required tracking tokens, not replacement approval categories. `APPROVED` requires a dated, verifiable decision reference. A written recommendation remains `RECOMMENDED` until the authorized owner explicitly approves it.
