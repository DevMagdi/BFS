## Status and authority rules

Use only these approval categories: **APPROVED**, **RECOMMENDED**, **PENDING**, **BLOCKED**, **NOT_APPLICABLE**. `APPROVED` requires explicit, verifiable approval by the authorized role. Technical code behavior, source-profile metadata, tests, GitHub checks, feature flags, and repository-maintainer roles are not owner approvals. A required role is described by responsibility below; the repository does not identify a person or authority for this use case.

The referenced implementation is evidence of a technical control only. Unless a row explicitly has a verifiable owner decision in the approval register, its approval status remains `PENDING`.

## Required accountable roles

No person, email, or delegated decision authority for this candidate is recorded in the repository. Required roles are:

- **Operations Business/Decision Owner:** owns the business objective, target definition, operational interpretation, acceptable error, consequences of a wrong forecast, current decision process, acceptance criteria, and review of results.
- **Source/Data Owner:** identifies exact source/fields and rights, grants purpose-specific authorization, explains coverage/freshness/corrections, and attests the source’s request semantics.
- **Data Governance and Retention Owner:** approves classification, minimization, lineage, retention/deletion, legal hold, and correction lifecycle.
- **Security/Privacy/Legal approvers:** decide access, sensitive-data boundaries, rights, security controls, and any applicable privacy/legal use constraints.
- **Evaluation Owner:** approves chronological design, holdout controls, metric interpretation, minimum valid windows, acceptance protocol, and claims of independent evidence.
- **Deployment Operator:** may configure an approved non-production deployment but gains no business approval authority by operating it.
