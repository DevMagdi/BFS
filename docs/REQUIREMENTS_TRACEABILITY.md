## External decisions that remain separate from technical acceptance

- **Retention:** The accountable retention/domain owner must decide if Operations history may ever be purged, the trigger/eligibility and legal-hold treatment, and how evidence, hashes, corrections, analysis runs, and source revisions remain verifiable. No duration is invented. Until then, technical restrictions remain in force.
- **Sensitive-data handling:** The accountable security/privacy/data owner must classify allowed real-world event fields and determine any additional handling before customer/production data use.
- **Source/business meaning:** An accountable Operations/source owner must select any authoritative source, permitted fields, completeness/freshness/correction expectations, and a recurring question.
- **KPI approval:** The accountable Operations owner must separately approve any KPI semantics, unit, inclusion rules, completeness/freshness conditions, period, target, or threshold. The initial count remains a technical observation, not an approved KPI.
- **Production authorization:** The accountable product/data owner and applicable security/privacy/legal approvers must authorize the specific data classes, source access, retention, deployment controls, and operating responsibilities.

These decisions block only the relevant business/customer/production use; they do not reopen the accepted four-pillar direction or the bounded Phase 1 engineering baseline. See [`architecture/PHASE1_SCOPE_ACCEPTANCE.md`](architecture/PHASE1_SCOPE_ACCEPTANCE.md) for the concise scope record and exact decision ownership.
